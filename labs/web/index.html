SRPG labs

d6 roll-under, from the short rules

Checks Character NPC Adventure Oracle

Browser labs

Plain HTML and JavaScript for the table. A check succeeds when the d6 lands below the attribute. Advantage keeps the lower of 2d6. Disadvantage keeps the higher.

Checks

Roll-under attribute checks, Advantage, Disadvantage, modifiers, attack and defense criticals, plus the travel, reaction, recovery, and coin rolls from the rules.

Character

Combat, Manual, or Knowledge. Attributes are a d6 plus the class modifier. Two scores may be swapped. Higher levels use the experience procedure.

NPC

A person from the three classes and the List RPG archetypes, or a creature from the D1–D7 tables.

Adventure

Roll a journey from the lists in the List RPG notes: background, objective, destination, encounters, boss, reward, and conclusion.

Solo oracle

A d6 yes/no oracle for play without a Game Master. Likely and unlikely use Advantage and Disadvantage. The travel watch uses the rules.

Open labs/web/index.html directly, or serve the repository. Relative links keep working on a static host such as GitHub Pages at labs/web/.

The Python files next to this folder are optional command-line sketches. Several of them roll a d20 or build OSR characters and are not SRPG. Read labs/README.md.

Rules live in rules/. These pages do not change them.
