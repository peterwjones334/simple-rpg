Attribute checks

Roll 1d6. Succeed if the result is below the attribute. A modifier is added to the die first, and a higher total is worse.

Dice
Kind of check

If Advantage and Disadvantage both apply, they cancel: roll a normal check. Harder foes add +1 to the roll for each Hit Die above the character's level. A natural 1 on an attack is a critical hit. A natural 6 on a defense is a critical failure.

Damage dice

Rolls from the rules

Open d6 pool

Hit dice, weapon damage, and any other handful of d6s. This does not apply roll-under by itself.

See Game mechanic and Advantage, initiative and combat. Travel and reactions are in Turns. Recovery is in Rest and healing.
