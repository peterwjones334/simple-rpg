Character

Roll the class table in order: STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, CHA. Two of those scores may be swapped. Hit points are a d6 plus STR, CON, and Armor Points.

Level 1 is the class table. Each level after that adds 1d6 to maximum HP, then rolls 1d6 against each attribute and raises it by 1 if the roll is higher. Focus, if you tick it, tries only STR and DEX for Combat, DEX and WIS for Manual, or INT and WIS for Knowledge.

No swap yet. One pair of creation scores may be swapped.

Roll a character to fill the sheet.
