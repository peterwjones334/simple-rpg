# How to play

Simple RPG (SRPG) uses paper, pencils, and six-sided dice. One person is the Game Master. Everyone else plays a character. This page is the order of play. The procedures it names are defined in the other files in this folder.

## What you need

- A Game Master and at least one player
- A pencil and a [character sheet](./character-sheet.md) for each character
- A handful of d6s
- An objective for the session, set by the Game Master

## Setup

1. Read the [game mechanic](./game-mechanic.md). A check succeeds when the player rolls **below** the relevant attribute on **1d6**.
2. Each player makes a character from [Characters](./characters.md): Combat, Manual, or Knowledge. Roll the dice on that class table. Two attributes may be swapped.
3. Write down starting gear from the class table and [equipment](./equipment.md). A new character begins with clothes, one day of rations, a one-handed weapon the class allows, and 1d6 × 10 coins.
4. The Game Master prepares places, opposition, and the objective. Creature entries are in [Creatures](./creatures.md). Spells are in [Books and spells](./books.md).

## A turn

Time and movement are in [Turns](./turns.md).

- Moments are for fights and other fast danger. Minutes are for exploration. The Game Master can jump the clock to hours, days, or longer.
- On a turn a character may move somewhere Nearby and take an action, or skip the action and move somewhere Far Away.
- An action is whatever the character attempts: attack, search, talk, cast. The Game Master names the attribute. Roll 1d6 and succeed if the result is below it.
- Advantage is 2d6, keep the lower die. Disadvantage is 2d6, keep the higher die. Both are defined in [Advantage, initiative and combat](./action.md).

## A fight

1. Each character checks DEX. Those who succeed act before their opponents, in an order they choose. Those who fail act after.
2. A melee attack requires the opponent to be Close, and the check is STR. A ranged attack is DEX. A ranged attack against a Close opponent is at Disadvantage.
3. Creatures do not make attribute checks. A character avoids a melee attack with STR and a ranged attack with DEX. Only the damage roll uses the creature's Hit Dice.
4. Armor Points reduce damage after a hit. On an attack, a natural **1** hits and doubles the damage dice. On a defense check, a natural **6** fails and the character takes double damage. Armor Points still apply.
5. At 0 Hit Points the character is Out of Action. When the danger has passed, roll on the [recovery](./recovery.md) table if the body can be recovered.

## Between fights

- About an hour of rest restores Armor Points.
- Once a day, after a rest, roll the class Hit Die and regain that many Hit Points.
- When the Game Master awards a level, follow [Experience](./experience.md): add 1d6 to maximum Hit Points, then roll 1d6 against each attribute and raise it by 1 if the roll is higher.

While travelling, the Game Master rolls 1d6 every 20 minutes of real-world play. On 1–2, the next turn brings an encounter. Reaction to that encounter is a 2d6 roll on the table in [Turns](./turns.md).

## When it ends

The session ends when the objective is met, or the characters are dead. Survivors can return in a later session in the same world.

Terms are collected in the [glossary](./glossary.md).
