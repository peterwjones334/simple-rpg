Core Rules

Roll 1d6. Succeed if lower than the relevant attribute.

Advantage: 2d6 keep lowest. Disadvantage: 2d6 keep highest.

Critical hit: natural 1 doubles damage dice. Critical failure on defense: natural 6 takes double damage.
