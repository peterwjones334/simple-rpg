# Sample Adventure: The Phylactery of the Black Fen

**Generated from the List RPG tables.**

## Background
**Dark Fantasy** — A bleak and grim setting with horrors lurking around every corner and moral ambiguity at every turn.

## Objective
**Retrieve an Artifact** — The players must recover a valuable and powerful artifact that has been lost, stolen, or hidden away.

## Destination
The Black Fen, a drowned woodland where the water never freezes and the trees grow black with rot. At its heart stands a half-sunken manor.

## Key NPC
**Mysterious Stranger** — A hooded figure who meets the party at the fen’s edge. They claim the phylactery must be destroyed before the next new moon, or the Witch of the Fen will complete her ascent.

## Journey Encounters (roll or use in order)
1. **Natural Obstacle** — A flooded causeway. Crossing requires a DEX or STR check; failure means lost rations and Disadvantage on the next check.
2. **Weird Encounter** — A group of chickens walking the road in boots and hats. They do not appear scared. They lead the party to a hidden cache if followed, or attack if mocked (1 HD each).
3. **Sinister Event** — The party’s shadows begin to lag behind. CON check or lose 1d6 HP as something feeds.
4. **Found Object** — A well-preserved ancient artifact: a silver knife that counts as a magical weapon against undead and were-creatures.
5. **Enemy Attack** — 1d6+1 Ghouls (2 HD) rise from the mud.

## Boss
**The Lich Queen** — A powerful undead sorceress who can raise the dead and drain the life force of her enemies. Her power is tied to a hidden phylactery that, if destroyed, weakens her.

**Stats (suggested):** 7 HD, Armor 6 (only silver or magic), Attacks: drain (1d6 + CON check or lose a level) or raise 1d6 zombies as an action. Phylactery is hidden in the manor’s well.

## Reward
Destroying the phylactery grants each surviving character +1 to a key attribute of their class and a permanent minor blessing (Advantage on one type of check of the player’s choice, once per day).

## Conclusion
The fen begins to drain. The Mysterious Stranger removes their hood, revealing they were the Witch’s former apprentice. They offer the party a place in the new order—or a head start running.
