# Field notes

These pages are table notes for **Simple RPG (SRPG)**. They are not the rules.

Play from the short chain in [rules/](../rules/index.md), starting at [How to play](../rules/how-to-play.md). A check there is roll-under on a d6. Classes are Combat, Manual, and Knowledge. Armor is Armor Points.

Parts 1–3 were generic guidebook drafts (“[Name of RPG]”, and in places a d20 attack roll). The text is still here. The voice is now a notebook sitting next to SRPG: where a paragraph teaches another game’s dice, treat it as hobby vocabulary, not as a procedure to use at this table.

The ~14 MB `TTRPG_Overview.pdf` is not in the repository. The same survey, without that file, is [TTRPG overview](./ttrpg-overview.md).

## The three parts

1. [Part 1 — The game](./part-1-the-game.md) — what a session is, and a fantasy toolbox (places, gear, creatures as prompts).
2. [Part 2 — Players](./part-2-players.md) — sitting down, making a person, and staying in the game.
3. [Part 3 — Game Masters](./part-3-game-masters.md) — running the table, safety, and prep.
